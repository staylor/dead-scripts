# Dead Sheets Privacy Policy

Dead Sheets doesn't collect, store or share personal information, and it has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

- **Sync with Other Devices** is off until you turn it on. While it's on, the song you open is saved to the private iCloud database of your Apple Account, which only your devices can read. Apple's iCloud terms apply.
- **Apple Music** access goes through Apple's MusicKit, after you allow it. Dead Sheets shows album artwork from the Apple Music catalog and plays songs from it, and doesn't read your library or listening history.
- **Apple Watch** receives the song you open directly from your iPhone.

Questions can go to [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/staylor/dead-scripts/issues).

Last updated October 2026.

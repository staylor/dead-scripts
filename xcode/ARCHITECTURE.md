# Architecture

```
xcode/
├── DeadSheetsKit/          Swift package shared by every target: the catalog and its logic
│   ├── Sources/DeadSheetsKit/
│   │   ├── Catalog.swift            seeds.json → value types, lookups, data problems
│   │   ├── Models.swift             Song, Album, Artist, Person, Credit, Role
│   │   ├── Search.swift             matching songs, albums, artists, singers, covers
│   │   ├── WriterGroups.swift       "By Writer" rows
│   │   ├── Credits.swift, Lyrics.swift
│   │   ├── WatchConnectivityManager.swift
│   │   └── Resources/seeds.json     generated by `pnpm xcode`
│   └── Tests/                       Swift Testing, including seed integrity
├── leadsheets/             iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV app (one multiplatform target)
│   ├── LeadSheetsApp.swift          scenes, commands, app delegates
│   ├── CarPlaySceneDelegate.swift
│   ├── Models/UserData.swift        favorites and setlists (SwiftData)
│   ├── Services/                    CloudSyncManager, MusicPlayerService, ImageLoader
│   ├── Intents/                     App Intents, Spotlight, Handoff
│   ├── Views/                       SplitRootView (iOS, macOS), TV/, Library/, Sheet/, Setlists/
│   └── Resources/                   asset catalog, album covers, band photos
├── leadsheets.watch/       watchOS app
├── leadsheetsUITests/      iPhone and Apple TV UI tests
└── Sheets/                 lead sheets: pdfs/, and schema.json, which maps
                            each song to its score in iCloud Drive
```

## Data

**The catalog** is read-only and bundled. `Catalog.bundled` decodes `seeds.json` once into value types keyed by slug. Songs carry the names and cover they show in lists, and `Catalog` answers the relationship questions (an album's songs in track order, a person's credits). A singer and a writer with the same slug are the same `Person`. Data errors such as an unknown writer are collected in `Catalog.problems` rather than dropped: the app logs them, and the tests require there to be none.

**User data** is favorites and setlists, in a SwiftData store of its own (`UserData.store`). They refer to songs by slug, so a new `seeds.json` never touches them. The models avoid unique constraints and give every property a default, so CloudKit sync could be added later. If the store can't open, the app falls back to an in-memory store instead of crashing.

**Settings and window state** use `@AppStorage` (keep screen on, sync, show lyrics on the Mac) and `@SceneStorage` (category, song filter, open song).

## Navigation

iPhone, iPad and Mac share `SplitRootView`, a two-column `NavigationSplitView`:

- The **library column** is a `NavigationStack`. Its root is `LibraryList` for the selected `LibraryCategory`, with `.searchable` and a filter on Songs (all, favorites, covers). Drilling into an album, artist, singer, writer group or setlist pushes a `LibraryRoute`.
- The **detail column** is `SheetView`: the PDF, a toolbar (Play, Lyrics, Favorite, Add to Setlist, Previous, Next), and `LyricsView`. On iPad and Mac the lyrics are an `.inspector` column, and on iPhone a sheet with detents. An iPhone in landscape moves the bottom bar's buttons to the top and puts the lyrics beside the sheet, hiding the sidebar of a large iPhone to make room.
- On iPhone the split view collapses into a stack, and `preferredCompactColumn` moves to the sheet when a song is chosen.
- `SheetPDFView`, a `PDFView` subclass on iOS and the Mac, fits the page to the column's width. When the column changes size, on rotation, as a Mac window resizes, or when the lyrics or sidebar open, it keeps the same part of the sheet at the top.
- Previous and Next follow the list the song was opened from (`openedFromSongs`), so a setlist plays in order. Pedals that send arrow keys do the same, through key commands on iOS and `onKeyPress` on the Mac.
- `SongCommands` builds the Song menu from focused values, so it acts on the frontmost window.

tvOS uses `TVRootView`: a tab per category, plus the system search tab. Albums are a grid of covers, and artists, singers and writers grids of round photos, which open `TVRouteScreen`, the artwork beside the songs. Songs push `TVSheetScreen`. It puts the lead sheet beside the lyrics; the remote scrolls whichever has focus, pressing zooms the sheet in two steps, and Back zooms out before it leaves. `ZoomableSheet` draws the PDF with Core Graphics in tiles, because tvOS has no PDFKit, so it stays sharp at every zoom. The watch shows the song list and lyrics, and opens the song chosen on the iPhone.

## Services

- `MusicPlayerService` plays Apple Music catalog songs with `ApplicationMusicPlayer`. It reads play/pause state from the player itself, so the state stays right when playback changes elsewhere. Failures become an alert, or the Apple Music subscription offer (`playbackAlerts()`). CarPlay uses the result to show its own alert.
- `CloudSyncManager` shares the open song through one CloudKit record in the private database. It makes no requests until sync is turned on. While the app is active it polls, one request at a time, honoring CloudKit's retry-after; in the background it relies on a query subscription's silent pushes. Devices are told apart by a per-install ID.
- `WatchConnectivityManager` (in the package) passes the selection between iPhone and Apple Watch.
- `SongIntents.swift` exposes songs as an `AppEntity` with an Open Song intent and App Shortcut phrases, indexes them in Spotlight, and defines the Handoff activity.

## Platform differences

| | iPhone | iPad, Mac | Apple TV | Apple Watch | CarPlay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sheet | PDFKit | PDFKit | Core Graphics tiles, zoomed with the remote | — | — |
| Lyrics | Sheet with detents, side panel in landscape | Inspector | Side panel | Scroll view | — |
| Setlists, favorites | Yes | Yes | — | — | — |
| Playback | Apple Music | Apple Music | — | — | Apple Music |
| Follows another device | CloudKit, watch | CloudKit, Handoff | — | iPhone | — |

Every platform but watchOS bundles `Sheets/pdfs`, a folder reference. `AppIcon.icon` is filtered out of tvOS, which uses Brand Assets.

## Conventions

- Swift 6 language mode, with main-actor isolation by default in the app targets. Delegate callbacks that arrive on other queues, such as `WCSessionDelegate`, are `nonisolated` and hop to the main actor.
- Observation (`@Observable`) rather than `ObservableObject` and Combine. Combine only bridges `ApplicationMusicPlayer`, which is an `ObservableObject`.
- `os.Logger` with a category per area (`Catalog`, `Sync`, `Music`, `CarPlay`, `UserData`, `WatchConnectivity`).
- Logic that doesn't need UIKit, AppKit or SwiftData goes in DeadSheetsKit, with tests.
- Rows set accessibility identifiers (`song-<slug>`, `album-<slug>`, …) for the UI tests.
- `swift format` with the repository's `.swift-format`.
